Balıkçı Market Support

Türkçe

Balıkçı Market is a local-first iOS fishing assistant for planning trips, saving personal fishing spots, reviewing weather-aware guidance, comparing selected photos with a fish guide, and keeping local catch records.

Contact Support

Use the public support tracker to open a support request: Balıkçı Market Support Requests.

When contacting support, include the app version and build number, iPhone model, iOS version, a short description of the issue, and the screen or flow where it happened.

Do not post sensitive personal information. Support will never ask you to post it in a public issue.

Common Questions

Does the app require an account?

No. The app has no account system.

Does the app upload fish photos?

No. Photo species review remains user-controlled: the user compares the selected photo with the guide and confirms the species before saving. The app does not upload the photo to a developer-operated recognition service or storage server.

Does the app guarantee a catch?

No. Weather-aware scores and species guidance are planning aids only. They do not guarantee fishing results.

Why does the app ask for location?

The personal fishing spots map can request when-in-use location to center the map near the user. Users can still use the app without granting location permission.

Where is my data stored?

The app stores personal fishing spots, catch and trip records, gear, preferences, related photos, and notes locally on the device.

How do I remove local data?

Deleting the app removes its local app data from that device. User-controlled iCloud or Finder device backups may retain a copy until the user deletes or replaces those backups. The developer does not hold account or server-side user data that can be deleted remotely.

Does the app need internet access?

Core local features work from bundled and locally stored data. Apple WeatherKit and Apple MapKit features may require network access for live weather and map services.

Safety and limitations

Fishing guidance is informational. Weather scores are not safety advice, navigation advice, or a catch guarantee. Users are responsible for following local fishing laws, weather conditions, and safety rules.
