FishingBuddy Support

FishingBuddy is a local-first iOS fishing assistant for planning trips, saving personal spots, reviewing weather-aware guidance, identifying fish from selected photos, reading a fish guide, and keeping local catch records.

Contact Support

Use the public support tracker to open a support request: FishingBuddy Support Issues.

When contacting support, include the app version and build number, iPhone model, iOS version, a short description of the issue, and the screen or flow where it happened.

Do not send sensitive personal information unless support specifically asks for it.

Common Questions

Does the app require an account?

No. The current MVP has no account system.

Does the app upload fish photos?

No. In the current build, fish identification runs on device using a bundled Core ML model after the user selects a photo.

Does the app guarantee a catch?

No. Weather-aware scores and species guidance are planning aids only. They do not guarantee fishing results.

Why does the app ask for location?

The Spots map can request when-in-use location to center the map near the user. Users can still use the app without granting location permission.

Where is my data stored?

The current MVP stores user-created spots, catch records, preferences, and related fishing data locally on the device.

How do I remove local data?

Deleting the app removes its local MVP data from that device.

Does the app need internet access?

Core local features work from bundled and locally stored data. Apple WeatherKit and Apple MapKit features may require network access for live weather and map services.

App Store Review Notes

Fishing guidance is informational. Weather scores are not safety advice, navigation advice, or a catch guarantee. Users are responsible for following local fishing laws, weather conditions, and safety rules.
